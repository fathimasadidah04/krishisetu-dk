
# 🌾 KrishiSetu DK

### A Voice-First Direct Market Access Platform for Marginal Farmers

KrishiSetu DK is a hackathon project designed to connect marginal farmers in Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka, directly with nearby buyers through voice-based produce listings, market-price benchmarks, and location-based matching.

## 🚩 Problem Statement

**DEVHOST 2026 — PS 1.2**

Farmers can face challenges accessing fair market prices, finding nearby buyers, and transporting small quantities of agricultural produce economically.

## 💡 Our Solution

KrishiSetu DK aims to make local agricultural markets more accessible through a simple, voice-first digital platform.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎙️ Voice-based produce listings in Kannada and Tulu.
- 📊 APMC market-price benchmarks.
- 📍 Nearby buyer matching for local shops, hotels, restaurants, and vendors.
- 🚚 Potential shared transportation for small orders.
- 📱 Mobile-friendly progressive web application.

## ⚙️ Planned Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js
- **PWA:** Workbox
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Uvicorn
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, PostGIS
- **Market data:** Agmarknet / government open-data APIs
- **Speech recognition:** Bhashini, subject to API availability

## 🔄 How It Works

1. A farmer submits a voice-based produce listing.
2. The system extracts the crop, quantity, and location.
3. Relevant market-price benchmarks are retrieved.
4. Nearby buyers are identified.
5. The farmer and buyer coordinate the sale and delivery.

## 📍 Target Region

Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka, India.
